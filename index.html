Welcome to Dronas

Growing together, every single day.

Dronas Public School is a private, co-educational school in Sadar, Betul, offering learning from kindergarten through upper primary. We celebrate the excitement of discovery and the importance of strong foundations.

Our aim is to create an encouraging learning environment where children can ask questions, develop confidence and explore their interests.
